FAQ

Common questions

Is the free plan really free forever?
Yes. The free plan has no time limit — just usage caps (50 expenses/month, etc.). You upgrade only when you need more.
What payment methods are supported?
We use Razorpay — UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, and wallets are all supported.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. Your data is stored with encrypted passwords, CSRF protection, rate limiting, and strict session policies. We never share your data.
What is the AI Insights feature?
It uses Google Gemini AI to analyse your last 3 months of transactions and generate 5 personalised, actionable recommendations specific to your spending patterns.
Can I export my data?
Pro users can export expenses, income, savings goals, and subscriptions to CSV — filterable by date range and category.
What happens when I hit the free plan limit?
You'll see a clear message explaining the limit and a link to upgrade. Your existing data is never deleted.
